# A/B: `bank-f23` (REVIEW-1 F2 + F3) vs the head it branched from (`0be83a4`)

**Outcome: F3 is free and TAKEN. F2's repair was MEASURED AND DECLINED — a
tightening, not a soundness repair, that costs contest deaths on `mixed` and so
does not clear the standing per-class bar
(`docs/design/decision-lens/08-DEPTH-VERDICT.md` §7.1). The branch ships F3
alone; `substrate.ts` is byte-identical to the head. This is the record of the
price, kept because the next attempt at F2 has to beat it.**

## The two changes

* **F3** — `bounds/bank.ts::price` gates on `base = withReferences(plan)`
  rather than on `plan`, so the footprint the entanglement gate tests carries
  the staged paths of teammates FIXED by reference. A held unit entangled only
  with such a path is enumerated instead of staying held.
* **F2 (measured, DECLINED, not on the branch)** — `substrate.ts::perilOf`
  probes a board without the FAMILY's modelled
  units as well as the view's own (`probeModelled()`). `withModelled` replaces
  the modelled set, so a B1/B3 view named the enemy alone and its probe board
  held OUR units: the peril of the other enemies then included what our own
  units could do to them, in every plan alike — the flattening `perilOf` exists
  to prevent, re-appearing at the B1/B3 branch scores.

## Method

Two worktrees, each built with `npx tsc -p .`:

* **before** = `0be83a4` (a clean `git worktree add --detach`, `node_modules`
  symlinked)
* **after** = `bank-f23` as measured, i.e. F3 AND the F2 repair. The branch's
  final state carries F3 only — F2 was reverted after this measurement, which
  is what the measurement decided.

`node dist/tests/local-game.js` in the deterministic node-budget mode
(`--nodes`, budget 550), 60 turns: `mixed`, `snakes`, `sparse`, `potions` at
seeds 1–3, `mixed` extended to seeds 4–6 because it was the only class that
moved, and `potions` extended to seeds 4–8. Compared with
`node scripts/ab-compare.js before after`, per class, never pooled.

## What moved

* **`snakes` and `sparse`: byte-identical.** Every counter is 0 on every seed.
* **`potions` (8 seeds): deaths `−0.022`/100** (2 seeds up, 2 down), meals
  `−0.449`, potion tier-ups `−0.394`. Inside noise in both directions.
* **`mixed` (6 seeds): deaths `+0.248`/100** — 3 seeds up, 1 down, 2
  byte-identical, all of it `deaths.contest` (`+1.0`/game, 3 seeds up and none
  down) — against **meals `+1.575`/100** (4 up, none down) and reversals
  `−0.397`.

**F3 is not what moved.** Built alone on top of `0be83a4`, F3 is
byte-identical on `mixed` at seeds 1–3: every reference action the runner
declares is `NO_ORDER_MOVE`, whose candidate carries an empty path and adds
nothing to the footprint. The whole delta above belongs to F2.

## Reading it

That trade is the finding's own prediction, arriving: an over-broad peril made
every enemy the plan could conceivably reach possibly-gone in every plan alike,
so a plan that TAKES a piece scored like one that ignores it. Taking our units
off the view's probe board gives the capture back its edge — the bot eats more
(`+1.575` meals/100) and dies more in contests (`+1.0`/game) reaching for it.

The direction of the OLD reading was safe (both endpoints widen — REVIEW-1 F2
says so, and the sixteen-arm gate agrees: `gate:exact` is 16/16 and no arm
prints an `INVERSION` before or after). So **F2 is not a soundness repair**: it
is a tightening that restores a distinction the flattening had erased. The
standing rule from §7.1 is that a change must be neutral or better per board
class, and on `mixed` this is not — so the repair was reverted and the finding
stays open and visible in REVIEW-1.md, with what a future attempt owes.
