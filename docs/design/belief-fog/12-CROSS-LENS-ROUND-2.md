## 1. The epoch sentence, reconciled (red team round 2, item 1)

Their law: "any mid-decision conditioning event starts a new epoch." My 09-doc
§8 disposition assumed observations land only at turn boundaries; 11-doc §B.3
then made C2 purchasable MID-decision — the two texts genuinely conflicted,
and the red team caught it. The reconciliation is drawn by the toll-free
theorem, and it splits their sentence down the channel boundary:

- **The sound channel needs no epoch.** A floor or ceiling priced over the
  wider S is a valid (looser) bound over the narrowed S; two sound numbers
  from either side of a mid-decision conditioning event are BOTH bounds over
  the current S and compare soundly. Witnesses are the one sound object that
  needs a membership re-check (a banked reply may have left S) — a set query
  on read, not an epoch.
- **The advised channel adopts their sentence whole.** est/mu priced under
  the pre-event (S, w) and post-event (S, w) are readings of different
  information states; comparing them silently is the laundering the refusal
  law exists for. Mechanism: a decision-local `conditioningEpoch`, bumped by
  any mid-decision conditioning; advised projections stamp it; the est rung
  and belief fold refuse-or-refresh on mismatch — and refresh is the
  kernel's EXISTING pattern (`refreshBelief` rebuilds the near half from the
  current triple), so the epoch is one integer and one comparison, not a
  teardown.

So: epochs for opinions, membership checks for witnesses, nothing for proofs.
Their sentence was right about the half of the system that has no theorem
protecting it.

## 7. C-B2, the partition on clouds — the third verdict covers structure too

Their ask: is a conditioning event one of the things that invalidates a
partition? The answer the toll-free law gives: conditioning never makes the
partition UNSOUND — entanglement computed on the wider clouds
over-approximates entanglement on the narrowed ones, so the coarse partition
remains a sound (merely coarse) grouping. What conditioning creates is an
OPPORTUNITY: a narrowing can split a component and make enumeration
affordable where it wasn't. So the partition joins the NARROWED verdict
class: survives-sound-but-coarse, flagged for profitable refresh —
`schedule = on-observation` as a priced lever in the operation set (09-doc
§4), never a mandatory teardown. Symmetric caveat, theirs to keep: DILATION
(each real turn, or thread advance) can MERGE components, and a stale-coarse
partition there is stale-WRONG in the unsound direction (two clusters
treated independent whose clouds now touch) — which their once-per-decision
schedule already handles at the turn boundary and threads handle by the
door's re-partition; the asymmetry (conditioning = optional refresh,
dilation = mandatory) is exactly the toll-free law's scope boundary and is
now stated in both lines.
