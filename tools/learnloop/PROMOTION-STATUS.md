*Closed in:* CLOSED. bin/make-promotion-batch.js derives N0`s cells from the union of what the batch schedules, and a generator check fails the batch if any treated cell is unfloored. ### CONTROL-CELLS-DEMOTE — **CLOSED**

applyMeasurement has no concept of a CONTROL cell. A cell that is null BY DESIGN — a provably-inert path included precisely to prove the instrument reports zero when zero is the truth — is indistinguishable to the rules from a treatment cell that failed to show an effect, and it takes the same `live-null` branch.

*Impact:* Demonstrated live during the batch-1 fold. TERRITORY_SLIDER_PROFILE's null-snake6 control sat at EXACTLY 0 against a measured +/-0.0324 floor — the strongest single row in the ledger, because it shows the measurement is not manufacturing effects — and filing it as family `placement` demoted the flag `supported` -> `live-null`. The better the control, the harder it demotes. This is a rule that punishes the practice it exists to encourage.
*Workaround:* The row is filed as family `audit`, which never moves a status and is arguably its true family anyway: an inert control is evidence about the INSTRUMENT, not a placement claim about the treatment. Recorded on the row itself so the choice is not mistaken for a dodge.
*Closed by:* On claude/cluster-lookahead: an explicit `control: true` on a measurement, refused from moving a status and rendered as a distinct badge; plus a selftest asserting that a passing control does not demote a supported flag. Until then `audit` is the correct filing and the note carries the reasoning.
*Closed in:* CLOSED. `control` is a fourth measurement kind. It enters no effect channel in either direction and accumulates on the flag`s `controlEvidence`; a control that MOVES is recorded under `violated` as an instrument failure, because a path required to read zero reading nonzero makes every treatment row beside it provisional. The slider`s row is re-filed as placement/control and no longer demotes; the selftest keeps the defect reachable by asserting that the SAME row filed as a placement null still demotes, so the fix is the kind and not a loosening of the null rule. cl/llfix on claude/cluster-lookahead — see lib/polarity.js, lib/ledger.js MEASUREMENT_KINDS, and bin/selftest.js sections 1b / 4.

*Impact:* Three flags are affected and their named experiments are unreachable from the generator: CENTAUR_UNIT_FATALITY (P7F — "is the classifier promotable independently of the flag that failed?"), CENTAUR_TIER_TRUTH (P4R, separately blocked on P11) and CENTAUR_COHORT_POLICY (P6R, separately deferred on counters). *Workaround:* None applied. P7F is named here so it is visible to a reader of the status page even though the generator will not emit it.
*Closed by:* On claude/cluster-lookahead, one of: include live-null flags whose last placement row is `underpowered` in undecided(); or add an explicit `scheduleAnyway` field to nextExperiment. The first is principled and the second is honest about being a manual override.
*Closed in:* CLOSED, by the first of the two options named here — the principled one. L.undecided() includes a live-null flag while its most recent live placement row is not adequately powered, so the decision is made on the evidence rather than on the status name. CENTAUR_UNIT_FATALITY`s P7F is scheduled in batch 2; CENTAUR_TIER_TRUTH`s P4R and CENTAUR_COHORT_POLICY`s P6R now surface as NOT SCHEDULED with their blockers named, which is visible where silence was. cl/llfix on claude/cluster-lookahead — see lib/polarity.js, lib/ledger.js MEASUREMENT_KINDS, and bin/selftest.js sections 1b / 4.

### ENGAGEMENT-SOURCES

ingest.js can read engagement from exactly one place: `health[].mechanism[<counter>]`, the CL7 block. AND THE SILENT REFUSAL NOW HAS AN ADDRESS (20260829, source-level at 8059b86): `territory.ts:388-416` runs an ALL-OR-NOTHING residency check — a single `-1` pointer, including one from a loop over every entry's `entEarliest[e]`, sends the WHOLE partition back to JS — against a FIXED-SIZE arena (`arena.ts`: memory is created with `initial === maximum`, and `alloc*` returns null when it is full), sized from a measured working set that a larger or slider-heavy roster overflows. That is the mechanism by which an arm can genuinely run and still read as not-engaged, it is the best available explanation for why the piece cells show less than the snake cell, and it converts "wasm may be refused on piece boards" into an arena-sizing bug with a file and a line. *Found by:* the same run, as the reason it wrote nothing.
*Exhibit:* CENTAUR_CLUSTER_SEED lost snake6 1.00 -> 0.15, 13-18x the measured floor, with plansEvaluated roughly doubling and 27 of 48 games ending in the subject's elimination where the off arm never lost a team. *Closed by:* a decision that is the owner's, not the loop's: either a second engagement channel (--engagement-evidence <metric> <arm-delta>, admitting a broken-arm diagnostic as evidence of running), or the acceptance that pre-CL7 batches are informative and never status-moving, in which case every row above is correctly refused and P5R/P7R are the only paths. ### CELL-QUALITY

THE LEDGER HAS NOWHERE TO RECORD HOW MUCH A CELL CAN MEASURE, so no rule can refuse a row for being asked on a board that cannot answer it. `headline-mix-king` — the batch's headline board, and the cell the wasm anomaly is reported on — is trajectory-unstable under ZERO treatment: the A/A null flips 26 of 48 placements between two builds of the SAME commit (54%), only 5 of 48 games come out identical, the paired turn-correlation is -0.193, A/A plans-per-decision swings +65%, and across 14 arm-sweeps of the identical configHash 3749a5dea5e82e99 the cap rate spans 0.229-0.458 (mean 0.306, sd 0.060) — batch 1's two wasm arms being exactly the minimum and the maximum of that spread. Pairing by seed buys NO variance reduction there. THE DESIGN QUESTION, WHICH IS WHAT THIS ITEM IS FOR AND ALL IT IS FOR: where should cell quality LIVE so that rules can consume it — a block on the batch record, a field on the A/A null's own rows, or a table the ingest derives and the generator reads — and WHICH RULES should it feed: refuse a placement verdict outright, require a larger n, or disqualify the cell from a spec at generation time? NOT DECIDED HERE, AND DELIBERATELY NOT BUILT. No `batches[].cellQuality` machinery was invented for it: the schema should follow the decision rather than lead it, and inventing a channel now would commit the loop to whichever of the three shapes happened to be easiest to write.

*Found by:* the 20260829 machine ingest and the 20260829 source-level replay-miner pass, independently and from opposite sides — the ingest from placement flips, the miner from game length.
*Impact:* Two-sided, and both sides are already in this ledger without being sayable. (1) EVERY PLACEMENT ROW ON THAT CELL IS NULL BY CONSTRUCTION. A 54% A/A placement-flip rate is what a +/-0.0973 score floor looks like in games, so the batch's headline board cannot carry a placement claim at n=48 — the rows recorded on it are honest and uninformative rather than wrong, and reading them as 'the treatment did nothing' overstates them. (2) NOTHING REPORTED THIS. The `flip-rate-rose` event fires only when the paired flip rate RISES against a previous batch, and batch 1 is the first batch, so a first-order fact about what the headline cell can measure passed through the whole instrument table in silence. That is FLIP-RATE-LEVEL seen from the data side rather than the rule side. By contrast `null-snake6` is stable — A/A 44 of 48 identical, and the order-preserving CENTAUR_UNIT_FATALITY holds trajectory correlation at +0.912 — which is exactly why P7's verdict on that cell stands while P5's on the mix-king cell does not. The quality of a cell is currently something a reader has to know; it should be something a rule can read.
*Workaround:* Taken where it costs nothing and is defensible one cell at a time: P5R drops `headline-mix-king` on this measured disqualification (see CENTAUR_WASM.nextExperiment.scopeNote), and every row is already read against its own cell's A/A floor since NULL-BAND-CELL-KEY closed, so an unreadable cell at least reads as unreadable. *Closed by:* the owner's decision on the design question above, then a schema field and one rule that consumes it.
