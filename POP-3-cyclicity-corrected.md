# POP-3 — CYCLICITY, CORRECTED: the instrument was circular, and the game is not transitive

Supersedes `POP-2` §2's conclusion. The librarian's correction (21-response @ 448f5dd) is right and
more general than my version, and acting on it **reverses the finding**.

---

## 1. THE CORRECTION

I concluded "cyclicity is unmeasurable by construction" and attributed it to the three-seat design.
**The real reason is more general and worse:** any `A[i][j] = m(i) − m(j)` built from per-arm
scalars is a **gradient field with zero curl by construction, at any seat count**. My pairwise
means were exactly that. So:

> **Fitting a Bradley-Terry-shaped matrix and finding no cyclicity is circular.** The zero was a
> property of the statistic, not of the game.

---

## 2. THE CURL-CAPABLE STATISTIC — and my first attempt at it over-claimed

The fix is an **ordinal** statistic, not a difference of per-arm means: `P(i finishes ahead of j)`
over co-seated games. Triangle sums are then not identically zero.

**First pass, raw probabilities — and it is wrong.** It reported large cycles (main triangle
−0.1146, p=0.000). But raw win probabilities are **bounded in [0,1] and saturate**: when territory
beats material 98% and reflex 99.8%, the edges pin at ±0.5 and a *perfectly transitive* triple is
forced to show a large "cycle". That is the same bounded-statistic trap that has now caught me
three times this session.

**The correct test is the LOGIT**, because Bradley-Terry is *exactly additive* in logit space, so a
non-zero logit triangle sum cannot be manufactured by a strong ordering. With a Haldane–Anscombe
correction and a within-game permutation null (200 draws):

| triangle | min n | **logit cycle** | perm-null p | null mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| parentDefault / potionIntel / reflex | 302 | **−0.634** | **0.000** | 0.092 |
| **material / territory / reflex** | **4841** | **−0.297** | **0.000** | 0.018 |
| parentDefault / reflex / searchOnly | 48 | +0.510 | 0.080 | 0.247 |
| plain / potionAware / potionBold | 72 | +0.448 | 0.070 | 0.193 |
| material / slider / reflex | 192 | −0.198 | 0.105 | 0.094 |
| *(six others)* | | | ≥0.245 | |

**Two triangles carry real, significant intransitivity**, including the best-powered one in the
archive at n=4,841 with a null mean of 0.018.

---

## 3. WHAT IT MEANS — smaller than "rosters must be mixtures", larger than nothing

**The game is not transitive.** That is now measured rather than assumed, and `POP-2` §2's
conclusion is withdrawn.

**But the magnitude is modest, exactly as the counterweight predicted.** A logit cycle of 0.30 is
an odds-ratio inconsistency of e^0.30 ≈ **1.35** — the triangle's win probabilities cannot be
generated by any single rating vector, but they miss by ~35% in odds, not by a sign flip. With
cyclicity this small the equilibrium of the ordinal game stays near-pure, so **this does not yet
license "rosters must be mixtures"**. Three-seat play genuinely suppresses rock-paper-scissors
because third parties absorb losses, and the numbers are consistent with that.

**The consequence that does bite is methodological, and it is visible per cell.** Main triangle,
logit cycle by board:

| cell | n | logit cycle |
|---|---|---|
| null-snake6 | 1284 | **+0.601** |
| c1-owner-snake | 120 | +0.430 |
| hazard-mix-king | 480 | +0.038 |
| headline-mix-king | 1290 | +0.017 |
| snake5-knight | 336 | −0.213 |
| snake5-queen | 516 | **−0.428** |
| c1-owner-queen | 72 | **−0.552** |

> **The cyclicity flips sign between snake boards (+0.60) and piece boards (−0.43 … −0.55), and
> pooling them cancels most of it** (pooled −0.297, mean per-cell magnitude far larger).

So a single pooled rating is **not a sufficient statistic** for this population, and per-cell
reporting is not a refinement but a requirement — the pooled number is an average of two opposite
regimes. This is the same snake-versus-piece seam every other instrument in my lens has landed on,
arriving now through the ordinal structure of finishing order.

## 4. FOLDING THE OTHER TWO REFINEMENTS

**(2) The VBS−SBS null carries a qualifier, and I have added it to `POP-1`.** The disjunction is
(a) there is no headroom, (b) there is headroom **the pool cannot express**, (c) underpowered.
`territory`/`slider` at **+0.996** and `plain`/`potionOrder` at **+0.879** are direct evidence for
**(b)** — much of the measured "headroom of selection" is the headroom of choosing between two
names for one thing. Under (b), **the architecture's pitch is not a better selector but the
ability to hold members that are not variations of each other**, which is precisely ruling 49's
state. The instrument should be a **standing column, re-run whenever a non-lineage member seats** —
that re-run is the experiment that discriminates (a) from (b), and nothing already on disk can.

**(3) Cross-cell profile correlation is adopted as the standing redundancy column**, in place of
Nash mass concentration, which degenerates on a near-transitive matrix. Read against the
−1/(k−1) co-seating null.

**And `reflex` winning `snake5-knight` is the dead-instrument fact's third independent detection** —
after the R1 score collapse and the elimination-rate floor. Three independent detections of one
property, each found by accident by a different instrument, is the argument for M26's automatic
detector: a cell whose outcome cannot move should be flagged *before* blocks are spent on it, not
rediscovered by whoever happens to look.

## 5. WHAT I WOULD STILL NOT CLAIM

- **Not "the pool needs mixed rosters."** Measured cyclicity is real but small; the mixture
  argument needs a bigger effect than 1.35 in odds.
- **Not that the two significant triangles are strategic in origin.** Board heterogeneity within a
  cell (seeds, spawn geometry) can also generate genuine ordinal cyclicity, and I have not
  separated that from strategy.
- **Not the raw-probability cycles from §2's first pass.** They are saturation artefacts and are
  superseded by the logit figures.
